# Community conduct

This project welcomes constructive participation regardless of background, experience, identity or writing genre. Discuss the work respectfully, provide evidence, accept corrections and protect people's privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, repeated personal attacks and deliberately exposing private manuscripts or credentials are unacceptable. Technical disagreement is welcome; targeting people is not.

This applies to project issues, PRs, discussions and other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may request edits, remove abusive content or temporarily/permanently restrict participation, proportionate to severity and recurrence. Anyone involved in a report should recuse themselves from deciding it.

For an ordinary conduct concern, ask for a private reporting route without posting sensitive details. Maintainers must establish and advertise that route before soliciting confidential reports. Do not publish allegations or personal information in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This is a project-specific initial policy, not a claim that a third-party code-of-conduct template has been adopted. Enforcement contacts and appeal arrangements remain in the [maintainer setup checklist](docs/development/maintainer-setup.md).
